THE PREDICTION

What we think will happen

Citrini Research published a prediction in February 2026: a chain reaction kicks off when AI starts eating into corporate-software revenue.

STEP 1
AI replaces software spend
Companies cancel SaaS subscriptions in favor of cheaper AI tools.
STEP 2
White-collar layoffs
Software companies and corporate buyers cut headcount as growth slows.
STEP 3
Crash
Consumer spending collapses, financial system strains, S&P 500 falls ~56%.
We believe the prediction is directionally right (this will happen) but the timing is delayed 6–18 months. So our job is to be positioned for it without bleeding to death waiting.
THE STRATEGY · TWO PHASES

We have two modes. Right now we're in Phase 1.

PHASE 1 · WAITING — WHERE WE ARE

Stay defensive while we wait

Hold a small, balanced portfolio. The biggest piece pays off if the prediction comes true. The rest is insurance.

Don't bet aggressively against the market until at least 2 of 5 economic readings cross.

PHASE 2 · ACTION — WHEN 2 SIGNALS CROSS

Switch to bear bets

Sell the AI-stock holdings. Replace defensive options with shorter, more aggressive bear bets. Add positions on commercial real estate and junk bonds.

Keep 15–20% cash for buying opportunities during bear-market rallies.

THE TRIGGER

Five economic readings tell us when to switch phases

When 2 or more cross their danger line, we move from Phase 1 to Phase 2.

01 Job openings drop below 6.0M for 2 months

03 Software growth slows ServiceNow & Workday both below 14%

LAYER 1 · THE MAIN BET

Software puts — the big payoff position

WHAT WE HOLD

Long-dated put options (contracts that pay off if a stock falls) on these enterprise-software companies:

NOW CRM HUBS WDAY DDOG

Expiring January 2027 and January 2028, with strike prices 20–30% below today's stock price.

HOW WE MANAGE THEM
  • If a put has fewer than 90 days until expiry, we replace it with the next January expiration (same size).
  • If a put doubles in value (2× entry), we sell 1/3.
  • If it quadruples (4× entry), we sell another 1/3.
  • The last 1/3 rides until either the prediction plays out or we hit an abort signal.
Why long-dated: we don't know exactly when the crash starts, so we buy contracts that won't expire before our prediction window closes.
LAYER 2 · INSURANCE

Safe havens — what holds value if we're wrong

TLT

Long-term US bonds

Rises when investors flee to safety, or when interest rates fall (which usually happens in a recession).

GLD

Gold

Holds value during inflation, currency stress, or financial-system shocks. The classic crisis hedge.

XLP

Consumer staples

Procter & Gamble, Coca-Cola, Walmart. People still buy soap and food in a downturn. Falls less than the broader market.

Why these: the prediction could be wrong about what breaks, not just when. If inflation comes back hard, or geopolitics shocks the market, these hold up while the software puts decay.

PHASE 2 · EXIT RULES

How and when we'll close out

RULE OF THIRDS (UPSIDE)

On any position that doubles or triples:

  • Sell 1/3 at 2× entry
  • Sell another 1/3 at 4× entry
  • Let the last 1/3 ride to thesis completion
TIME-BASED ROLLS

Any option with fewer than 30 days until expiry that's still in profit gets rolled forward (closed and replaced with a longer-dated version) so we don't lose value to time decay near expiry.

TARGET COMPLETION

The original prediction calls for the S&P to fall ~56% from its peak. When we get within striking distance of that level, we close out and move to cash. Don't be greedy — take the win.

SIGNALS REVERSE

If the 5 readings start moving back the other way (jobs recovering, software growth re-accelerating), we close bear positions progressively rather than holding into a recovery.

THE ABORT SIGNAL

When we close everything

If the economy moves opposite to our prediction, we don't keep doubling down. We close every position and walk away.

2 or more of these "abort triggers" active = close everything.

A1 Job openings re-accelerate above 8.0M for 2 quarters
A2 Software growth re-accelerates ServiceNow > 18%, Workday > 15%, both rising
A3 Unemployment stays low into 2027+ below 3.8%
A4 Money velocity climbs & savings stay low M2 velocity > 1.45 + savings rate < 5%
A5 S&P 500 sustains above 8,500 multiple monthly closes

When the abort triggers, the agent emits a single CLOSE EVERYTHING recommendation and stops suggesting new trades. The user manually closes positions.

SAFETY STOPS

Built-in guardrails

Even if the strategy is working, the agent has hard limits that pause new trades to protect the account.

DAILY LOSS STOP

−4% in a single day

If the account loses more than 4% of its equity in one trading day, the agent stops opening new positions. Existing positions are untouched. Owner approval is required to resume.

ABORT TRIGGER

2+ anti-prediction signals

When 2 or more abort triggers are active, the agent suggests closing every position. The user always has the final say.

APPROVAL REQUIRED FOR
  • Switching from Phase 1 to Phase 2
  • Any trade larger than the standard size
  • Buying a ticker we haven't traded before
  • Closing every position at once
  • Restarting after the safety stop fires

THE PHILOSOPHY

Be positioned, not committed.

We're not betting the whole account on one prediction. We're holding a small, balanced portfolio that survives any of three outcomes:

RIGHT & ON TIME  →  software puts pay off, we rotate to bigger bear bets

RIGHT BUT LATE  →  AI longs cushion the wait, puts roll forward

WRONG IN A DIFFERENT WAY  →  bonds, gold, staples hold the line; we abort cleanly

The agent reads the data three times a day, sends a digest, and recommends moves. You always have the final say. The job of this plan is to make those recommendations easy to act on — not to make decisions for you.
